Welcome to my website! I am a Software Engineer at Mason with 7+ years of experience across e-commerce, healthcare, insurance, and commodities trading.

Originally from an Aerospace Engineering background, which I still enjoy teaching when I can, I pivoted into Data Science a few years ago and eventually made it my full-time focus. I have industry experience working as a Data Scientist at Flipkart, India's largest e-commerce company.

I hold an M.S. in Data Science from the University of Colorado Boulder, where I researched using diffusion models to generate efficient multi-agent warehouse layouts that improve throughput. I was affiliated with the [CAIRO](https://cairo-lab.com/lab.html) (Collaborative AI and Robotics Lab) and [HIRO](https://hiro-group.ronc.one/) (Human Interaction and Robotics Group) labs, applying reinforcement learning and neural networks to real-world problems.

Beyond work, I love running, dancing, playing the guitar, and hitting the gym.

The best way to reach me is my email. I am always down to collaborate and discuss my work or yours if I am knowledgeable about it.
